# Threats to validity

Results are hardware-, driver-, framework-version-, workload- and measurement-backend-specific. Software estimates do not equal meter readings. Background processes, DVFS and thermal state can add noise. Five pairs is a minimum and may have low statistical power. Identical math does not guarantee identical kernel selection. Classical CPU and deep-learning GPU comparisons confound algorithm and device. Composite scores depend on declared weights and remain secondary to raw trade-offs.
